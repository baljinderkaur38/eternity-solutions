PSA Gas Generator

Compressed air is passed through a twin tower PSA module interconnected with automatic change over valves, after passing through this bed of Activated alumina air gets dried. Supply of dry compressed air from this layer of desiccant (Activated Alumina) will be continuous without any interruption, dried compressed air will now come in contact with bed of carbon molecular sieves (CMS), molecules of oxygen, moisture & other unwanted gases are adsorbed on surface of CMS and nitrogen which is not adsorbed by CMS comes out of adsorption tower. The two towers of PSA modules are interconnected with automatic changeover valves, the outgoing pure gas is sent to a surge vessel where the minimum gas pressure will be maintained with the help of back pressure regulator, the produced gas will be sent to the consumer point through a pressure reducing valves at required pressure.

Features
  • Constant Supply of Nitrogen
  • Guaranteed Purity
  • High Performance
  • Low Pressure Drop
  • Safe & Reliable Operation
Range
  • 5Nm3/Hr to 300Nm3/Hr
  • Purity: 99% to 99.999%

Oil Free Screw Air Compressors

Conventional Oil Injected Screw Air Compressors carries residual oil vapours, which is not permitted in critical manufacturing applications of Pharmaceuticals, Electronics, Semi-Conductors, R&D Laboratories, Food & Beverage. Customer can’t take a risk of contamination of lubricating oil from compressor resulting failure of products, increased downtime & compliance with authorities. Deep Pneumatics offer 100% Oil Free Water Lubricated Screw Air Compressor to ensure complete peace of mind with no risk of oil carry over along with compressed air. By using our 100% Water Lubricated Screw Air Compressor, customer achieves highest quality of compressed air in compliance with ISO8573-1 Class 0.
