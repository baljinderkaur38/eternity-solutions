Meet Our Trusted Partners

We collaborate with industry leaders to deliver top-tier solutions.

Deep Pneumatics

Deep Pneumatics is a leading manufacturer of high-quality compressed air systems and accessories in India. With a commitment to innovation, performance, and energy efficiency, Deep Pneumatics provides advanced solutions that meet the evolving demands of industrial applications. From oil-free screw compressors to precision-engineered air dryers and accessories, their product range ensures reliability, productivity, and sustainable performance across industries.

Quick Air

Quick Air, a part of the renowned Canara Hydraulics group, is a leading manufacturer of air compressors and pneumatic equipment in India. Known for delivering precision-engineered, energy-efficient compressed air solutions, Quick Air combines decades of experience with modern technology. Their robust product range includes lubricated and oil-free compressors, screw and piston compressors, and custom pneumatic systems designed to meet industrial demands across various sectors.

Carl Stahl Craftsman

Carl Stahl Craftsman Enterprises Private Limited, established in 2007 as a joint venture between Carl Stahl GmbH, Germany and Craftsman Automation Limited, India, emphasizes affordability and quality. They offer lifting solutions that enhance component handling and assembly processes, resulting in increased production. Designed and manufactured in India to European standards, their systems feature streamlined operations and ergonomic design, ensuring a smooth and effortless lifting experience. With a focus on safety and efficiency, their products create a seamless and secure work environment, empowering businesses to grow more efficiently.
